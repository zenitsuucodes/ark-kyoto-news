大相撲：大野里が2勝リードを維持、青錦は直近3人のライバルの中で

Yokozuna Onosato kept his two-win lead at the Autumn Grand Sumo Tournament on Thursday as ozeki Aonishiki, new sekiwake Fujinokawa and former ozeki Asanoyama all won to stay in the title race. Seeking his first Emperor's Cup since last September, Onosato (11-1) stood firm against an opening charge from Atamifuji (8-4) and secured a right underarm belt hold while moving slightly backward before flooring the new sekiwake with a pulling throw. It was a damaging defeat for Atamifuji, who needed 12 wins at this month's 15-day meet to reach the usual ozeki promotion requirement of 33 wins over three straight meets fighting as sekiwake or komusubi. Back at the second-highest rank of ozeki, Ukrainian star Aonishiki (9-3) quickly held a belt hold with his left hand and spun around No. 10 maegashira Kinbozan (8-4) from Kazakhstan in a force-out win at Tokyo's Ryogoku Kokugikan. Fujinokawa (9-3) overcame ozeki Kirishima (6-6), who had a slight edge in a shoving battle until the sekiwake sidestepped to his left at the edge. Fujinokawa failed to push him out from the back but used his left arm well as he chased after Kirishima around the ring before claiming a frontal-crush-out win. No. 6 maegashira Asanoyama (9-3) allowed an early belt hold to Churanoumi (8-4) but immediately forced it off before forcing out the No. 3 maegashira. Ozeki Kotozakura (8-4) shoved out No. 5 maegashira Oshoma (6-6) to secure a winning record.
