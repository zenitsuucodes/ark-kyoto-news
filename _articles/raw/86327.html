MAPPA EXPO is Officially Open! Step Into The World of Jujutsu Kaisen, Attack on Titans, Chainsaw Man

By TK Ogawa,Animate Times
- Sep 28, 2026 - 08:00 All

The MAPPA EXPO 15th Anniversary is set to take over the YURAKUCHO MUSEUM in Tokyo from Wednesday, September 16, to Monday, December 7, 2026.

Ahead of the public opening, an exclusive media preview and opening ceremony were held, featuring actress and artist Non—a familiar face to MAPPA fans—who made an appearance as the exhibition’s official ambassador.

In addition, new venue photos and a floor map have been released. These teasers showcase highly anticipated installations, including the fateful phone booth from “Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc,” the hillside scenery from “Attack on Titan,” and the specific room where Gojo Satoru took a nap in “Jujutsu Kaisen.”

<The following content is quoted from the official announcement>

 

 

Non Takes the Stage as Exhibition Ambassador at the Opening Event!

 


Prior to the public launch, a private preview was held for industry insiders and media. The highlight of the event was the appearance of actress and artist Non, who has been appointed as the “MAPPA EXPO 15th Anniversary” Ambassador.

Non’s history with MAPPA runs deep; she famously voiced the protagonist Suzu Hojo in the MAPPA-produced film “In This Corner of the World.” Reflecting on her feelings when she first landed the role, she shared, “Even though it’s animation, the world felt so real, as if the story truly existed. I remember thinking that if I didn’t get to voice Suzu-san, I would regret it for the rest of my life.”

This film was also her first major project after transitioning to her current stage name, “Non.” She described the timing as being “born at the same time as Non,” noting the deep personal significance of the project. “I knew it would be a special work for me as I took this new step as an actor. I felt like it was my life’s mission to play this role,” she added, showing her profound connection to the character.

 

 

When asked about her favorite MAPPA titles, Non listed “Jujutsu Kaisen,” “Yuri!!! on ICE,” and “Hell’s Paradise: Jigokuraku.” She praised MAPPA’s unique creative DNA, saying, “Their work is so wonderfully detailed. I love how you can just lose yourself in those worlds. No matter which title you watch, there’s a sense of style. The clever direction, the camerawork, the fluidity—it’s all incredible. Watching it just gives me chills; it’s so cool.”

While Non previously associated MAPPA most strongly with the grounded, artistic style of “In This Corner of the World,” she admitted to being blown away by the studio’s recent evolution. “I was shocked! I thought, ‘Wow, they had this side to them too?!'” she laughed. “They’ve been producing so many high-octane, stylish action series lately that it really took me by surprise. Every time they release something new, it’s exciting.”

Regarding the exhibition’s theme, “Right Beside the Story,” Non expressed her resonance with the concept. “It’s a lovely theme. As a fan, you always want to be near the stories you love. I’m moved by how much this exhibit thinks about the fans’ wishes,” she said. “Animation starts with a single line, and from there, colors, movements, and expressions that don’t exist in the real world are born. Being able to feel that work ‘beside you’ provides a sensation you just can’t get from watching a TV or movie screen.”

Sharing her impressions after touring the floors, Non’s excitement was palpable. “It was wonderful. There are raw original drawings on display, and several sculptures and art installations being shown for the first time. I mean, if you’re a fan, you simply have to come here!”

She also highlighted that photography is allowed in almost every area of the exhibit, making it a dream for social media. “There are so many Instagrammable spots,” she noted. Regarding the immersive nature of the displays, she raved, “It’s fantastic. You can focus entirely on the work without any distractions. It’s the kind of exhibit where you can just clear your mind and let the world of the anime take over.”


When the topic turned to the limited-edition merchandise, Non picked out a few items herself. “The designs are stylish, and the color palettes are beautiful. They’re subtle enough that you can easily wear or use them in your daily life,” she recommended.

To wrap up the event, Non left a message for MAPPA fans: “To celebrate MAPPA’s 15th anniversary, there are so many new works on display, and the art installations are truly magnificent. It’s an exhibit that makes you feel wrapped up in the world of the stories. Please, come and experience it for yourselves.”

 

 

MAPPA EXPO 15th Anniversary: Finally Open to Public!

 


This exhibition is designed as a special experience where fans can immerse themselves in the charm and atmosphere of MAPPA’s legendary catalog, featuring “Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc,” “Attack on Titan: The Final Season,” and the “Jujutsu Kaisen” anime. In addition to the displays, fans can purchase limited-edition goods featuring exclusive illustrations and original production art.

New Exhibits & Floor Map Revealed for Key Areas!

 


We are excited to unveil new details regarding the specific installations in the “Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc,” “Attack on Titan: The Final Season,” and “Jujutsu Kaisen” areas.

Please note: Some exhibition content is scheduled to be rotated between the first and second halves of the event’s run.

“Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc” Area

 

“The Fateful Phone Booth”
The iconic location where Denji and Reze first met, setting their fates in motion. This area features a full-scale recreation of the phone booth from the film, allowing fans to step directly into the atmosphere of the movie.

 

“Attack on Titan: The Final Season” Area

“The Creation of the Hillside Scenery”
The unforgettable background of the hill Mikasa visits at the end of the story was brought to life through beautiful watercolor paintings. This exhibit features a recreation of the production desk used at the time, allowing visitors to compare the production materials with the finished wall-sized art to see the step-by-step process of how a single landscape is born.

 

“Jujutsu Kaisen” Area

“The Room Where Gojo Slept”
An area modeled after the room in Tokyo Jujutsu High where Gojo Satoru was seen sleeping in Episode 29 (Hidden Inventory arc). Based on the actual layouts from the show, this space is designed to make fans feel as though they have stepped right into the world of the anime.
