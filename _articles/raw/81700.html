Foreign visitors to Japan parliament hit record high in 2025

KYODO NEWS
- Aug 06, 2026 - 19:54 All,Japan,Travel/Tourism

TOKYO - The number of foreign visitors to Japan's parliament building reached 13,697 in 2025, the highest since record-keeping began in 1993, according to data compiled by both houses of the Diet.

The number, nearly doubling from 7,261 in 2024, comes amid surging inbound tourism, with a record 42.7 million foreigners visiting Japan last year.
