Asian Games: Olympic winners Oka, Hashimoto miss all-around gold

By Morichika Nakamoto,KYODO NEWS
- Sep 21, 2026 - 19:42 All,Asian Games,Sports

NAGOYA - Japan's Shinnosuke Oka had to settle for silver and Daiki Hashimoto for bronze as China's Zhang Boheng defended the artistic gymnastics men's individual all-around gold medal at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Monday.

Paris Olympics winner Oka and Tokyo Games champion Hashimoto both made landing errors and scored below 14 points in their final apparatus, floor exercise, while Zhang finished with a 14.700 on the horizontal bar to seal the title at Nagoya General Gymnasium.
