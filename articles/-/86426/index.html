Baseball: Murakami homers twice as White Sox beat Rockies

ARK KYOTO NEWS
- Sep 26, 2026 - 14:22 全て,スポーツ

CHICAGO - Munetaka Murakami homered twice, raising his rookie-season total to a team-high 35, as the Chicago White Sox beat the Colorado Rockies 6-1 on Friday.

Murakami hit solo shots in the first and seventh innings off Rockies starter Tomoyuki Sugano (12-12) at Rate Field.

The former Yakult Swallows slugger also added an RBI single in the eighth and finished 3-for-4 with three RBIs.

A former Yomiuri Giants ace, Sugano allowed four runs on six hits, including Murakami's two homers, while striking out five over 6-2/3 innings.

"I went to the plate with a different image (of Sugano in Japan). I battled hard and managed to get some hits," Murakami said of the veteran pitcher, whom he previously faced in Japan's Central League.

"We all share the goal of winning the division. Building on our work every day is very important, and I'll do my best again tomorrow."

Kazuma Okamoto hit two run-scoring doubles in the Toronto Blue Jays' 6-5 win over the Cincinnati Reds.

Okamoto, who has hit 33 home runs in his rookie season, finished 2-for-3 with three RBIs at Rogers Centre.

In other MLB action, Roki Sasaki worked a scoreless eighth inning in relief as the Los Angeles Dodgers defeated the San Francisco Giants 2-0.

His Japanese teammate, Shohei Ohtani, who recently returned from the injured list, went 0-for-4 with three strikeouts at Oracle Park.
