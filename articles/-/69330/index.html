FEATURE: Sailor Moon's magic endures as new theater set to open in Tokyo

By Donican Lam,スポットライトジャパン
- Jan 29, 2026 - 15:00 全て,日本,特徴,スポットライト

More than 30 years after Pretty Guardian Sailor Moon first sparked a global phenomenon and established the fighting magical girl genre, the franchise continues to evolve with the opening of a new permanent theater in Tokyo staging performances throughout the year. Pretty Guardian Sailor Moon -Shining Theater Shinagawa Tokyo-, set to open in Shinagawa Prince Hotel's Club eX in April, will carry on the legacy of Pretty Guardian Sailor Moon -Shining Moon Tokyo-, a theater restaurant which opened briefly in 2019 in the Azabu-Juban district, the setting of the series in Tokyo's Minato Ward.
