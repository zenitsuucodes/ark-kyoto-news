Asian Games: 15-yr-old skateboarders among 8 golds for Japan

ARK KYOTO NEWS
- Sep 25, 2026 - 18:17 全て,アジア競技大会,スポーツ

NAGOYA - Japan's 15-year-old sensations Mizuho Hasegawa and Izuru Nagahara won the skateboarding park events on Friday as the host bagged eight gold medals overall on a productive day at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games.

Top in the world rankings, Hasegawa eased to victory after earning 94.03 points with the second of her four final runs at Aichi Sky Expo Hall. Lin Yi-fan of Taiwan was second on 85.15 and Japan's Misugu Okamoto third on 82.86.

"I really enjoyed it and was able to perform close to my best," said Hasegawa, whose 88.83 from her first attempt would have been enough for the win.

In the men's bracket, Nagahara scored 88.96 to edge Taiwan's Ku Yong-yan who scored 87.33. Nagahara's compatriot Gunjo Shiji took bronze on 86.03.

"It hasn't quite sunk in yet," Nagahara said. "My older brother (Paris Olympian Yuro) got silver last time, and I was gunning for gold here...The view was great (from the podium)."

Nagoya native Yuri Tanaka became the first Japanese winner in the women's heptathlon after topping its final two events, the javelin and 800 meters.

"It's not just the Asian Games for me, I've had a very strong emotion toward this competition. This gold medal is huge," Tanaka said.

Misaki Morota also became the first Japanese winner in the women's pole vault after rewriting her own national record by clearing 4.55 meters.

Aiko Sugihara won the women's floor exercise gold to add to her individual all-around triumph earlier in the games, becoming the first Japanese female artistic gymnast to win two golds.

In fencing, the Japan men's foil team won gold after the Paris Olympic champions beat Hong Kong 45-36 in the final, while in swimming, Kodai Nishiono won the men's 200-meter backstroke in 1 minute, 56.42 seconds.

The host nation also beat the Philippines 21-11 in the final of the women's 3x3 basketball and has 28 gold medals so far, second only to China, which has 98.
